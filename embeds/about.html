Harshal Tholambia is a Communication Designer from the National Institute of Fashion Technology, raised in Kota, Rajasthan.

He’s drawn to imperfections and always on the lookout for narratives worth telling. The work is rooted in visual storytelling and a love for playing with colour with the aim of keeping a little magic alive in it all. Most of it comes from personal experiences and moments he wants to hold onto. Art is just his way of capturing them.

ps. he makes better art when the music’s loud.✌️
